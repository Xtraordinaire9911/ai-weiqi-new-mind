Credit is required when you distribute or publish this project, a modified version, or anything built from its data or documents. Put the credit line below where your users will see it, for example in the README, on the about page or in the footer of a site, in a video description, or in the references of a paper. 如果你做了修改，请在署名行后加一句简短说明，例如“经 <你的名字> 修改”，并保留两个许可的链接（https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0 和 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/）。署名的写法不得让人以为本项目或作者为你背书。
